## Information stored by the app

Ritli stores the information needed to provide its features on your device:

- Tasks, including titles, notes, categories, priorities, due dates, estimates, and completion or archive status.
- Timer routines and preferences, including focus and break lengths, appearance, notification choices, and Lock Screen task-name visibility.
- Focus and break session records, including dates, durations, status, associated tasks or categories, and the time zone used for the record.
- Your onboarding completion state and current timer progress.

The app uses this information to run timers, organize tasks, restore timer state, and display your history and statistics. You do not need a Ritli account.

Ritli does not upload these app records to a server operated by us. The app contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDK, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. It does not sell your task or timer data.

## Notifications and Live Activities

If you allow notifications, Ritli schedules timer alerts locally through iOS. These alerts use general focus or break messages and do not include your task titles or notes. You can change notification permissions in iOS Settings.

Ritli uses Live Activities to display timer information on supported system surfaces, including the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. Task names are hidden by default. If you enable **Settings → Privacy → Show task names on Lock Screen**, the selected task name may be visible to anyone who can see those surfaces. Turn this setting off to hide task names. You can also manage Live Activity permission in iOS Settings.

## Retention and deletion

Local records remain in the app until you delete them or remove the app's data. Archiving a task retains it. You can delete an archived task from **Tasks → Archived Tasks**; its recorded sessions remain in history without that task relationship.

Use **Settings → Data → Clear focus history** to delete recorded completed, cancelled, and skipped sessions. This keeps your tasks, preferences, and any running or paused session.

Deleting Ritli from your device removes its local app data. Offloading the app preserves its documents and data. Device backups may retain earlier copies and may restore them later. Manage backups through your device, iCloud, or computer backup settings. We do not have access to those backups or a server copy of your local task and timer records.

## Apple services and device backups

Apple provides iOS, notifications, Live Activities, the App Store, and optional device backup and diagnostic services under Apple's terms and privacy policies. Depending on your settings, app data may be included in device backups. Ritli does not provide its own cloud synchronization service.
